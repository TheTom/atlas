Throughput is the arithmetic mean of the three recorded rates. Each displayed latency is the mean of the three **per-repetition** percentiles, not a percentile pooled across requests. C1 has only one measured request per repetition, so its p50 and p99 coincide and do not establish a tail distribution. The frozen harness restarts prompt nonces across processes, and both engines enable prefix caching; warmup, earlier gates and retained caches can affect reuse. Raw repetition series and a machine-readable calculation are in the [measurement summary](evidence/rental-h100-20260905/rental-overview/MEASUREMENT-SUMMARY.json), [Atlas ladder](evidence/rental-h100-20260905/rental-overview/runtime/qwen38.atlas.c.lat.c1/ladder.json) and [vLLM ladder](evidence/rental-h100-20260905/rental-overview/runtime/benchmark.qwen38.vllm.lat01/ladder.json).

The profiles differ materially. Atlas serves a 24,576-token context, FP8 KV with 256 calibration tokens and automatic high-precision layers, active batch 4, 128 sequence slots and SLAI scheduling. vLLM logs a 262,144-token context, BF16 model dtype, `kv_cache_dtype=auto`, 512 sequence slots and enabled chunked prefill. Both use one GPU and prefix caching, but these capacity and precision settings are not a matched configuration. Atlas starts from the same FP8 checkpoint and requantizes some dense projections and the LM head to NVFP4 for its default runtime paths; the repaired Hopper path preserves its supported W4A16 fallback. vLLM logs FP8 quantization. Equal checkpoint bytes do not imply equal runtime arithmetic. A separate native-FP8 Atlas investigation is pending; `ATLAS_DENSE_FP8=1` alone has not yet proved that all batched FFN and LM-head routes avoid requantization. No same-precision performance claim is made.
